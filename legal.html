Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Aura shows you who is nearby and what they are listening to. To do that it has to know roughly where you are and who is in the same room. This page explains exactly what Aura collects, why, who can see it and how long it is kept.

The short version

  • Other people only see you while you have Aura open on your screen, never while ghost mode is on, and not after you tap Leave the room.
  • Your exact position is never shown to anyone. Distance and direction appear only after you have both waved.
  • Your room position is deleted within about 20 minutes of leaving. Chats with people who are not your friends are deleted after 24 hours.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Choosing your gender and who you want to see in Dating mode can reveal information about your sexual orientation. Aura uses these settings only to filter your radar and never shows them to anyone. Why we are allowed to use it

  • To provide the service you asked for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)): your account, profile, music, being in the room, waves and chats.
  • Your consent (art. 6(1)(a) and art. 9(2)(a)): location and Bluetooth permissions, Apple Music access, Last.fm, and the Dating mode settings. You can withdraw consent in your phone's settings or in Aura.
  • Legitimate interest in safety (art. 6(1)(f)): blocks, reports, the 18+ check, and keeping evidence when someone breaks the rules.
  • Legal obligations (art. 6(1)(c)): answering valid requests from authorities.

How long it is kept

  • Room position and Bluetooth readings: removed from the radar 45 seconds after you close Aura or leave the room, and deleted from the server within about 20 minutes. Your last precise position is kept for at most one hour, only to spot fake locations.
  • Waves and chats with people who are not your friends: deleted 24 hours after the first wave or the match.
  • Chats with friends: kept until one of you removes the friendship or deletes the account.
  • Contact details you shared: kept until you stop sharing them, the friendship ends, either of you blocks the other, or an account is deleted. Your details are not stored anywhere else on the server.
  • Profile, photo and music: kept until you change them or delete your account. When you change your photo, the old one is deleted.
  • Blocks and reports: kept until either account is deleted.
  • Deleted accounts: removed immediately from the live database. Encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider roll over within a few days.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase hosts Aura's database, sign-in and photos in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Google handles Sign in with Google, if you choose it. Aura receives only your name and email address.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and Apple Music access. When you search for a song or artist, your search words are sent to Apple's public music catalog. No account details are sent.
  • Spotify, YouTube or Apple Music: when you share a song link to Aura, Aura opens that public link to read the song title and artist.
  • Last.fm, only if you connect it: Aura reads your recent tracks using your username.

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Where data leaves the EU, it is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to how it is used, or restrict its use. Most of this you can do yourself in the app: edit your profile, turn on ghost mode, block people, or use Profile › Delete account. For anything else, email the address above and you will get an answer within 30 days.

If you think your data has been misused, you can complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), or the authority in the EU country where you live.

Age

Aura is only for people aged 18 and over. Accounts with a birth date under 18 can't be created. If you believe someone under 18 is using Aura, report them in the app or email us and the account will be removed.

Security

All traffic is encrypted. Each person can only read their own data and what others in the same room are allowed to see; the database enforces this, not just the app. No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach affects you, you will be told as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, Aura will tell you in the app before the change applies. The date at the top always shows the current version.

Common questions

Nobody appears on my radar

Everyone must have Aura open on screen. Check that Bluetooth and Location are on and allowed for Aura in Settings. In Dating mode, your gender and age filters must fit both ways; switch to Vibe to see everyone.

How do I show the song I'm playing?

On Spotify or YouTube Music, open the song, tap Share and choose Aura. If Aura isn't in the list, tap More and switch it on. On Apple Music, go to Profile › Music › Connect Apple Music once and Aura picks it up by itself. You can also search for a song in Aura.

Why can't I see where someone is?

Distance and direction appear only after you have both waved. Until then you only see "very close", "same room" or "nearby".

How do I hide?

Turn on ghost mode on the radar. You disappear from everyone's radar straight away but can still chat with people you are already connected with.

Why did a chat disappear?

Chats with people who are not your friends are deleted after 24 hours. Tap Add friend in a chat to keep it; it stays once you both agree.

How do I block or report someone?

Open their profile and tap Block or Report. Blocking is instant. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I give a friend my number or Instagram?

Once you are friends, open your chat and tap Share contact. Choose WhatsApp, calls and texts, and/or Instagram. Only that friend sees it, and you can change or stop it from the same button.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile › Delete account. Your profile, photo, chats and connections are deleted immediately.
